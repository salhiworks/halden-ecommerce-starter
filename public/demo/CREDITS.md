# Demo photography

Every image in `public/demo/` is sample content for the demo store. They are
free stock photographs used under their sites' licenses — the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) — both of which allow free
commercial use and modification without attribution. They were cropped,
resized and stripped of metadata for this project, and retouched where a
maker's logo, label or print showed (see Notes); none of them comes from a
real business, and none shows a brand.

Replace them with your own product photography: add photos to products from
the dashboard, then use **Settings → Demo content → Remove demo content**.
You can then delete this folder.
